## E1 last-block adaptation, one configuration

Official pinned OxWearables HarNet10 Git `150550ea5d41800229c95e36f88f5bf0d2e7cf04`, `mtl_best.mdl` SHA-256 `c64f9135d99e2dcdfc9ae7cc0672f2bcc438df9ceb8215665882f92cddd162a6`. SSL raw Acc preprocessing and fixed first/last 10 s windows are identical to Phase-3B. HarNet `feature_extractor.layer1`–`layer4` are frozen eval-mode. Cache their per-window output [512,3] by subject/activity/wrist/window, without labels or fitted cohort statistics. `layer5` is the sole unfrozen external block; its conv and BN affine parameters are trainable, BN running statistics stay frozen in eval mode. Its per-window 1024D output is averaged with the same fixed window counts, then passed through the same Phase-3B LayerNorm(1024)+zero-initialized Linear(1024,64) wrist residual. STR wrist encoder, bilateral fusion, activity attention, structured residual and final classifier remain identical. Base/projection AdamW LR = 2e-4; layer5 LR = 2e-5 (exact 1/10). Weight decay = 1e-4 both. Cosine schedule uses original 50-epoch form and corresponding per-group minima 1e-6 / 1e-7, preserving the 1/10 ratio. No encoder LR search. Check stage4→original layer5 features against the Phase-3B final cache before training; verify layer1–4 and BN running means have no updates, only layer5 gradients, zero-residual logits match original STR at initialization; when loaded with the Phase-3B WSSL checkpoint, stage4→layer5 logits match the frozen WSSL on the same input, and smoke checkpoint reload. Run all 45 inner-development units.

Retain E1 only if seed-first 15-split BA improvement vs Phase-3B B has positive mean, >=10/15 positive splits and bootstrap 95% lower bound >0; AUROC mean >=0 with lower 95% bound >-0.01; Macro-F1 mean >=0, DD Recall drop <=0.01, PD Recall drop <=0.01, BA/AUROC within-split seed SD <=1.25x frozen B, and threshold disagreement not materially worse. Accuracy-only or AUROC-only gains do not retain. This stricter explicit gate makes the requested decision reproducible.

## E2 train-only OOF threshold, independent diagnostic

For each of 15 target inner splits and each seed, build 3-fold stratified subject-level cross-fitting **inside that split's training subjects** with fixed shuffle seed 20260930+3*outer+inner, identical OOF partitions for all three model seeds. In each crossfit fold, fit frozen Phase-3B WSSL from scratch on only the other two folds, recomputing train-only class weights and normalization. Use a fixed 10 training epochs (the Phase-3B B median best epoch), original AdamW/cosine trajectory with original T_max=50, and save/predict the epoch-10 model. OOF labels are never used for checkpoint/epoch selection. This deliberately sacrifices some fit to obtain genuinely held-out train-subject scores without using the target validation labels. Each training subject receives one OOF DD probability per seed. Choose a threshold on the fixed grid 0.05:0.01:0.95 by maximizing OOF BA, ties broken by closest to 0.5 then lower threshold. Apply seed-specific thresholds to the existing Phase-3B B validation probabilities. Also average the three subject-aligned OOF probabilities, choose one ensemble threshold by the same rule, and apply to the existing three-seed validation probability ensemble. No validation threshold search. AUROC is unchanged; report BA, Macro-F1, Accuracy, PD/DD Recall and the threshold distributions. E2 never trains a new final candidate or alters Phase-3B checkpoints. A threshold rule is retained only if single-model or ensemble BA improves on >=10/15 splits with positive 95% CI lower bound, Macro-F1 does not decline in mean, and PD Recall does not decline by >0.02; otherwise keep 0.5. OOF/validation score-distribution shift and smaller crossfit training sets are explicit limitations.

## E3 one middle+final SSL representation, only after E1 completes

Middle feature is HarNet layer4 output [512,3] averaged over time then over the same fixed windows to 512D. Final is the original frozen Phase-3B 1024D feature. Concatenate exactly [middle512, final1024] → LayerNorm(1536)+zero-initialized Linear(1536,64) → same STR wrist residual. HarNet fully frozen. All STR downstream and original training recipe unchanged. Compare only against Phase-3B final-only frozen B on 45 matched units. No layer, pooling, projection or fusion search. E3 retention uses the same BA/AUROC/Macro-F1/recall/variance gate as E1; if not met, stop multi-level features.

## E4 subject-count learning curve, mechanism only

For each inner train split, sample nested subject-level 25/50/75/100% subsets stratified by PD/DD using fixed random seed 20260930+3*outer+inner; subsets identical across model variants and seeds. Validation remains the same. At each fraction, compare original STR and Phase-3B frozen WSSL with the original recipe, class weights and normalization newly fitted only on that subset. The 100% runs are the already archived Phase-2 STR and Phase-3B WSSL runs; do not retrain them. Thus only 25/50/75% require 270 runs. E4 is not a model-selection contest. Report six metrics, 15-split paired WSSL−STR differences by fraction, bootstrap CIs, seed SD and split-level interaction/trend; do not treat fractions or 45 seed-runs as independent. A larger SSL advantage at low fractions is supporting development-only evidence of small-cohort benefit; otherwise report the null result.

## Statistics, evidence and decision

Three seeds aggregate inside each split; 15 fixed splits are the primary paired units. Use 10,000 split-bootstrap resamples, positive-split counts, paired Cohen dz and BH-FDR for prespecified BA/AUROC E1/E3 and BA E2 families. Report both cross-seed mean SD and mean within-split seed SD, score Spearman and 0.5-threshold disagreement. Subjects, windows, OOF folds, seeds and multiple fractions are not independent replicates. The original FOE-01 outer remains sealed for model development; nothing here may revise its frozen result. Final candidate is frozen B unless E1 or E3 passes its gate; if both pass, choose the one with higher paired BA then AUROC without a further search. E2 threshold rule is assessed separately from model architecture. Stop SSL adaptation if no candidate passes. All positive/null/negative findings and limitations must be appended to README and handoff.
