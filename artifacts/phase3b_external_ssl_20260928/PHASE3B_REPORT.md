# Phase-3B External Wearable SSL Transfer Study — final development report

Date: 2026-09-28. **Development-only.** This report uses the fixed 15 inner-development subject splits and seeds 42/43/44. The locked FOE-01 outer predictions/results, H1, handcrafted features, teacher/student distillation and any outer-based threshold or model choice were excluded. Formal STR-01, its training recipe, Phase-2/3A archives and FOE-01 were not modified. This is frozen transfer only; no pretrained encoder fine-tuning was run. [The protocol](PHASE3B_PROTOCOL.md) and [manifest](manifest.json) were frozen before the full matrix.

## Encoder, input adaptation, and audit

The sole external model is the official OxWearables `ssl-wearables` HarNet10 at Git commit `150550ea5d41800229c95e36f88f5bf0d2e7cf04`, checkpoint `mtl_best.mdl` SHA-256 `c64f9135d99e2dcdfc9ae7cc0672f2bcc438df9ceb8215665882f92cddd162a6`. All 131 feature-extractor keys matched the official loader; the randomly initialized official classifier was not used. The frozen extractor maps 3-axis accelerometry `[N,3,300]` at 30 Hz to 1024D. Its 10,457,408 parameters were never in the optimizer. Source: [official repository](https://github.com/OxWearables/ssl-wearables), [hub loader](https://github.com/OxWearables/ssl-wearables/blob/main/hubconf.py), [data preprocessing](https://github.com/OxWearables/ssl-wearables/blob/main/data_parsing/README.md).

PADS original wrist Acc XYZ is in g at 100 Hz. It was read from raw timeseries, not the STR L1-detrended, six-channel, train-normalized input. After the fixed first-48-sample trim, raw Acc was clipped to ±3g and anti-alias resampled by polyphase 3/10. A 2000-sample record used fixed first/last 10-second windows (two feature vectors averaged); a 976-sample record was edge-padded 12 samples per side (one window). No label, validation result or fold statistic selected a window or transformed the frozen cache. Acc-only SSL and Acc+Gyro STR paths retained the same subject → ordered 11 activities → left/right wrist mapping.

Raw mapping audited **390 subjects × 11 activities × 2 wrists = 8,580 records**, yielding 10,920 windows and finite `[390,11,2,1024]` pretrained/random caches. The cache SHA-256 is `f87829558938c3e50f78e53fe2f54156fc0f3768be3f73f97b089c0587c25376`. The random-frozen control used the identical HarNet10 extractor architecture, fixed initialization seed 20260928 and identical raw windows. Cache embeddings were joined by subject ID. Split-specific learned normalization was only the original STR train-only normalization; the external branch had no cohort-level normalization.

A (SSL-only) trained a 1024→64 wrist projection, original bilateral 258D construction, ordered 11×16D projection and linear subject classifier (72,146 trainable parameters). B and C kept the full 75,524-parameter STR architecture and inserted a zero-initialized 1024→64 residual at each 64D wrist embedding before the unchanged bilateral/activity/structured/final path (143,172 trainable parameters, equal between B/C). B used pretrained-frozen SSL; C used random-frozen SSL. All three used the original STR balanced CE, optimizer, scheduler, early stopping and fixed 0.5 DD decision threshold. No fusion/head/learning-rate/model shopping occurred.

Three smoke runs (A/B/C, seed42 split0.0) passed training, validation and best-checkpoint reload. The formal Phase-2 STR checkpoint under zero-initialized B residual gave **bitwise identical logits**, maximum absolute difference 0 on identical input; cached SSL features had `requires_grad=false`, while the projection received gradients. Training-source SHA-256 checks passed after the matrix. The complete matrix was **135/135** candidate inner runs; each matched the baseline's validation subject IDs/labels and train-only normalization SHA, with unique prediction IDs, saved checkpoint and `outer_test_loader_created=false`. The original Phase-3A E2 ensemble metrics were independently reproduced from baseline subject-aligned probabilities to numerical precision. Details: `analysis/extraction_audit.json`, `analysis/zero_residual_test.json`, `analysis/phase3b_integrity.csv`, `source_sha256.txt`, `smoke_*.log`, and `audit.log`.

## Seed-first single-model results

Each row is the mean of three seeds within each split, then the equal-weight mean over 15 fixed development splits. PD=0, DD=1. These are validation metrics, **not outer evaluation**.

| Original STR-01 | 0.7307 | 0.6972 | 0.7176 | 0.6861 | 0.7779 | 0.6165 |
| A: pretrained SSL-only | 0.7348 | 0.6949 | 0.7300 | 0.6865 | 0.7911 | 0.5987 |
| B: STR + pretrained-frozen SSL | **0.7456** | **0.7196** | **0.7596** | **0.7066** | **0.7823** | **0.6569** |

A achieved AUROC >0.5 in all 15 splits (range 0.6712–0.7980), indicating transferable PD/DD signal in the frozen external representation. Relative to STR, A's BA difference −0.0023 (6/15 positive; 95% CI [−0.0129,+0.0095]) and AUROC difference +0.0123 (9/15; [−0.0006,+0.0257]) were unstable; its DD Recall fell −0.0179. It is a feasibility result, not a retained classifier. There is no random SSL-only comparator, so its standalone signal cannot by itself isolate pretraining from all architectural effects.

B versus original STR: paired BA **+0.0225** (11/15 positive; split-bootstrap 95% CI [+0.0094,+0.0362], Cohen dz 0.815, primary BH q=0.0134), AUROC **+0.0419** (15/15; [+0.0298,+0.0541], dz 1.693, q=0.0002), Macro-F1 +0.0205 (12/15; [+0.0036,+0.0376]), DD Recall +0.0404 (11/15; [+0.0064,+0.0752]), and PD Recall +0.0045 (8/15; [−0.0337,+0.0391]). Accuracy rose +0.0149, but its CI crosses zero. Secondary metrics are descriptive and were not subjected to the eight-test primary BH family.

B versus equal-capacity C: BA **+0.0334** (14/15; [+0.0222,+0.0449], dz 1.442, primary BH q=0.0005), AUROC **+0.0506** (15/15; [+0.0366,+0.0652], dz 1.718, q=0.0002), Macro-F1 +0.0285, DD Recall +0.0695. C itself was below original STR on BA (−0.0110; 4/15 positive) and AUROC (−0.0087; 4/15). Thus the benefit is not explained by residual insertion or extra trainable projection capacity alone; the fixed pretrained representation contributes. This is a controlled attribution within the current PADS development design, not causal proof of which external pretraining factor matters.

## Seed variance, score agreement, and E2 ensemble reference

Across the three seed-averaged global scores, B versus STR seed SD fell for BA **0.0067 vs 0.0117**, AUROC **0.0018 vs 0.0120**, and DD Recall **0.0359 vs 0.0408**. More locally, the mean within-split three-seed SD fell for BA **0.0216 vs 0.0300** (paired difference −0.0084, 95% CI [−0.0136,−0.0033]) and AUROC **0.0217 vs 0.0323** (−0.0106, [−0.0177,−0.0039]). DD Recall within-split SD was 0.0785 vs 0.1024, but its paired CI crossed zero. Three-seed pairwise DD-score Spearman increased **0.6000→0.7734** (15/15 split increases, paired CI for +0.1734 [+0.1358,+0.2093]); fixed-threshold disagreement decreased **23.72%→17.80%** (−5.92 percentage points, CI [−9.60,−2.34]). B also exceeded C on seed score agreement. This supports reduced development seed-dependent prediction variance; three seeds give an imprecise variance estimate.

For a separate inference-level comparison, each model's three DD probabilities were averaged by subject ID before computing validation metrics; no threshold was tuned. The original STR E2 ensemble AUROC **0.7586** was exactly reproduced. A/B/C ensemble AUROC was **0.7414/0.7816/0.7389**; B's ensemble Accuracy/BA/Macro-F1/PD Recall/DD Recall was **0.7573/0.7200/0.7141/0.8100/0.6301**. B ensemble minus original STR ensemble AUROC was **+0.0230** (8/15 positive; bootstrap 95% CI [+0.0044,+0.0420]); BA difference +0.0202 had CI [−0.0029,+0.0438]. Ensemble AUROC adds another +0.0221 relative to B single-model seed-first AUROC 0.7596, but these are different estimands; the primary retention gate uses single-model seed-first results. B's single-model AUROC itself approximately matches the prior E2 ensemble 0.7586, while maintaining higher DD Recall than the prior E2 0.5816.

## Decision and limits

The preregistered frozen-transfer gate is **PASS**: B exceeds both original STR and C in BA and AUROC with positive means, ≥10/15 improving splits and bootstrap lower bounds >0 for all four primary comparisons; Macro-F1 and DD Recall rise, PD Recall does not materially fall, and BA/AUROC seed SD decreases. The BH adjustment covers the eight prespecified BA/AUROC paired comparisons (A/B/C vs baseline and B vs C); secondary claims are descriptive. Subjects, 45 seed-runs, 10,920 windows and PD–DD pairs were not treated as independent statistical units. Split bootstrap CIs summarize the repeated development scheme; the 15 splits share PADS subjects and are not an independent external cohort.

**New best pure-deep development configuration: B, STR-01 + frozen pretrained HarNet10 wrist residual.** The archived formal STR-01 and FOE-01 outer conclusions remain unchanged. The positive B result supports planning **one** separately registered Phase-3C minimal adaptation experiment, choosing either last-block unfreezing or a lightweight adapter with smaller encoder learning rate. This report does **not** execute that training, does not justify full fine-tuning or another external-model search, and cannot claim outer generalization for B. No FOE-01 outer result may be reused to select or debug Phase-3C.

Full per-run metrics, 15-split means, paired effects/CIs/BH, ensemble analysis, seed stability and prediction disagreement are in `analysis/`. The only new code is in this independent Phase-3B artifact's `scripts/`; prior formal model and results were not overwritten.
