A achieved AUROC >0.5 in all 15 splits (range 0.6712–0.7980), indicating transferable PD/DD signal in the frozen external representation. Relative to STR, A's BA difference −0.0023 (6/15 positive; 95% CI [−0.0129,+0.0095]) and AUROC difference +0.0123 (9/15; [−0.0006,+0.0257]) were unstable; its DD Recall fell −0.0179. It is a feasibility result, not a retained classifier. There is no random SSL-only comparator, so its standalone signal cannot by itself isolate pretraining from all architectural effects.


B versus equal-capacity C: BA **+0.0334** (14/15; [+0.0222,+0.0449], dz 1.442, primary BH q=0.0005), AUROC **+0.0506** (15/15; [+0.0366,+0.0652], dz 1.718, q=0.0002), Macro-F1 +0.0285, DD Recall +0.0695. C itself was below original STR on BA (−0.0110; 4/15 positive) and AUROC (−0.0087; 4/15). Thus the benefit is not explained by residual insertion or extra trainable projection capacity alone; the fixed pretrained representation contributes. This is a controlled attribution within the current PADS development design, not causal proof of which external pretraining factor matters.


For a separate inference-level comparison, each model's three DD probabilities were averaged by subject ID before computing validation metrics; no threshold was tuned. The original STR E2 ensemble AUROC **0.7586** was exactly reproduced. A/B/C ensemble AUROC was **0.7414/0.7816/0.7389**; B's ensemble Accuracy/BA/Macro-F1/PD Recall/DD Recall was **0.7573/0.7200/0.7141/0.8100/0.6301**. B ensemble minus original STR ensemble AUROC was **+0.0230** (8/15 positive; bootstrap 95% CI [+0.0044,+0.0420]); BA difference +0.0202 had CI [−0.0029,+0.0438]. Ensemble AUROC adds another +0.0221 relative to B single-model seed-first AUROC 0.7596, but these are different estimands; the primary retention gate uses single-model seed-first results. B's single-model AUROC itself approximately matches the prior E2 ensemble 0.7586, while maintaining higher DD Recall than the prior E2 0.5816.


The preregistered frozen-transfer gate is **PASS**: B exceeds both original STR and C in BA and AUROC with positive means, ≥10/15 improving splits and bootstrap lower bounds >0 for all four primary comparisons; Macro-F1 and DD Recall rise, PD Recall does not materially fall, and BA/AUROC seed SD decreases. The BH adjustment covers the eight prespecified BA/AUROC paired comparisons (A/B/C vs baseline and B vs C); secondary claims are descriptive. Subjects, 45 seed-runs, 10,920 windows and PD–DD pairs were not treated as independent statistical units. Split bootstrap CIs summarize the repeated development scheme; the 15 splits share PADS subjects and are not an independent external cohort.
