# Phase-2 development boundary and initial plan

Date 2026-09-26. Phase-1 STR-01 architecture remains fixed. All work uses the formal 5 contexts × 3 inner subject-level splits, seeds 42/43/44, training-fold-only normalization, PD=0/DD=1, full bilateral 11-activity input. The FOE-01 outer artifact, predictions, labels, metrics, and test signals are out of scope and are not used for model selection, tuning, augmentation design, or debugging. The original STR/V8/H1 archives remain read-only. New outputs live only in this directory.

Before any new candidate, reproduce all 45 STR-01 inner runs with the exact existing architecture and recipe. Match the original formal predictions/metrics and early-stopping epochs, and inspect train/validation losses. If reproduction fails, diagnose before proceeding. The standalone runner calls only the existing `_run_inner_fold` routine; it does not call `run_nested_training`, which would also execute outer-final evaluation.

Phase-2 comparisons use 15 split-level seed-aggregated values, paired to the reproduced STR baseline. Primary BA and AUROC, secondary macro-F1, accuracy, PD/DD recall, seed SD. Prespecified decision discipline: a candidate is not selected from a single seed/split or Accuracy alone; favorable evidence requires nontrivial paired gains on BA and AUROC in a majority of splits, macro-F1 improvement, DD Recall improvement without material PD Recall loss, and no material seed variance increase. Negative and ambiguous outcomes remain archived. Candidate screening and confirmation remain inner-development only. No outer result can resolve ties.

The previously rejected V8-GN LR=1e-4, batch16, WD=1e-3, temporal dropout0.2, and constant schedule are historical V8-specific negatives; Phase-2 does not reopen V8 or backbone search. STR-specific training coverage will be bounded and single-factor. Before adding any augmentation, assess current loss/early-stopping and validate physical semantics of the proposed transformation. Augmented records stay within the original training subject and do not increase independent subject count. No GAN, diffusion, raw long-sequence SMOTE, reversal, large rotation, or arbitrary time permutation.

After exact baseline reproduction, `manifest.json` records the bounded single-factor candidates before candidate results are inspected. Hyperparameter checks: STR LR 1e-4, WD 1e-3, label smoothing 0.05. Batch 16, temporal dropout 0.2, constant schedule, and LR 3e-4 were rejected in the earlier V8-GN study; they are not repeated as a broad STR grid. Epoch 50/patience 12 are assessed from all baseline loss trajectories and cap-hit counts; extra epochs or patience are not run absent evidence of undertraining. AdamW is retained; changing optimizer and scheduler alongside these limited comparisons would make the study substantially broader. Loss/sampling checks are separate: unweighted CE, sqrt inverse-frequency CE, balanced-weight focal gamma 2, effective-number CE beta 0.99, and a train-only balanced subject sampler without augmentation. The original train-balanced CE is the reference. Each variant changes exactly one factor/family relative to baseline.

Single augmentation candidates: mild normalized jitter SD 0.01, raw-signal amplitude scaling factor N(1,0.03) clipped to [0.94,1.06] before re-standardization with training-only statistics, smooth endpoint-preserving time displacement at most 1% of task duration, and conservative same-label input mixup with 0.9≤λ≤1.0 only when activity/wrist masks and lengths match. Each is tested separately with (a) equal 0.5 perturbation probability for PD and DD training subjects and (b) DD-only perturbation probability 1.0 for DD and 0 for PD. The latter changes the share of augmented exposures but does not change the number of distinct DD subjects or original class-sampling probability; the balanced-sampler-only control separately tests altered PD/DD exposure. Every epoch's PD/DD exposure and augmented/original counts are logged. Input mixup is exploratory because combining two distinct phenotypes may be physiologically implausible; no blend of multiple augmentation operators is allowed unless at least two single operators meet the joint gate on the full three-seed, 15-split analysis.

The prior formal STR checkpoints and split hashes are checked for exact reproduction. Full checkpoint binary SHA may differ because the run name/provenance changes; model-state tensors, best epochs, predictions, and metrics are the reproducibility criteria. Baseline: 45/45 best epochs identical, 45/45 model-state tensors bitwise identical, maximum DD probability difference 0, BA/AUROC 0.697189/0.717631. Best epoch 6–31 (median 12), none at the 50-epoch cap; mean train/validation loss at selected epoch 0.339/0.715 and at stopping 0.041/1.087. This provides no evidence for insufficient training; late training instead shows a widening loss gap.

Operational retention gate for this bounded study (specified after baseline reproduction and an interim script check of the already-complete LR 1e-4/equal-jitter results, both negative on the joint endpoint; before the remaining matrix was inspected): seed-first mean ΔBA≥+0.005 and ΔAUROC≥+0.005 versus reproduced STR, each positive in at least 10/15 paired splits; mean ΔMacro-F1>0 and positive in at least 10/15; mean ΔDD Recall>0 with at least 9/15 positive; mean ΔPD Recall≥−0.010; BA/AUROC fold-bootstrap 95% CI lower limits >0; BA and DD Recall across-seed SD no more than 25% above baseline. BH q values over the prespecified primary BA/AUROC family are reported as multiplicity context, not used to rescue a candidate failing the gate. For an augmentation combination, at least two single operators must individually pass the entire gate; only then one pair of the passing operators may be evaluated, with no arbitrary policy search. If no candidate passes, the development-only best configuration remains the reproduced STR-01 recipe.
