# PURE-DEEP PHASE-3 DEVELOPMENT: frozen plan

Date: 2026-09-28. This phase is inner-development only. The formal STR-01 architecture, fixed 5×3 subject-level inner splits, seeds 42/43/44, train-only normalization, 11 activity and bilateral wrist order, final DD decision threshold, batch, scheduler and early-stopping rule are preserved. Prior FOE-01 outer data, metrics, and predictions are not consulted. H1 and handcrafted/statistical models are excluded from every training, representation, selection, and threshold operation in Phase-3A. Phase-2 and formal archives are read-only; new outputs are isolated here.

E1 audit conclusion before new work: Phase-2 balanced_sampler applies WeightedRandomSampler to training subjects and does not override `loss.class_weights: train_balanced`; therefore it combines balanced sampling with balanced CE. The missing control is balanced sampling + unweighted CE. No sampling-ratio search.

E2 is a deterministic post hoc inference operation on the Phase-2 exact-reproduction STR-01 inner validation predictions. For each split and subject ID, average DD probabilities across seeds 42/43/44 before computing six metrics. Report it separately from the mean of three per-seed metrics. It is an inference strategy, never a new single model.

E3 changes only optimizer step geometry. Fixed SAM rho=0.05, L2 perturbation, AdamW base optimizer and original LR/WD/scheduler/batch/balanced CE. Two forward/backward passes per batch; no rho search. Selection uses the same validation BA and early-stopping rule. Check original final logits at zero perturbation, no BatchNorm in STR model, and checkpoint reload.

E4 adds a training-time auxiliary branch at the 11×258D pre-aggregation bilateral activity representation: shared Linear(258,32) → ReLU → Linear(32,2) per activity, masked mean over valid activities, then a single subject-level auxiliary balanced CE. There is no activity-label CE. L_total=L_STR+lambda*L_aux; fixed lambda values 0.1 and 0.3, with lambda 0 matched by exact Phase-2 STR baseline. The original STR final logits alone determine validation predictions and checkpoint selection. The auxiliary branch is disabled at inference and its outputs are excluded from final predictions. No ranking/prototype/contrastive/gating/attention/augmentation addition. An eval-mode logits consistency test must verify an unchanged STR backbone gives bitwise identical final logits before full training.

Statistics: three seeds aggregated first within each of the 15 fixed splits; split-level paired differences are primary. Report six metrics, split and seed SD, improvement counts, 10,000 split-bootstrap 95% CI, paired standardized effect size, and BH-FDR for the primary BA/AUROC family. Seed runs, subjects, activities and pairs are not independent samples. Keep a candidate only if BA shows a stable paired increase on most splits, AUROC increases or is credibly non-decreasing, Macro-F1 and DD Recall do not deteriorate, no Accuracy-only PD-favoring trade-off, and seed SD does not materially increase. For operational clarity: positive BA mean and ≥10/15 improving splits with bootstrap CI lower bound >0; AUROC mean≥0 and bootstrap CI lower bound >−0.005; Macro-F1 mean≥0; DD Recall mean≥0; PD Recall mean≥−0.01; BA/AUROC seed SD≤1.25× baseline. This gate is fixed before E1/E3/E4 validation results. E2 is reported separately as a fixed inference strategy and is not used to choose E1/E3/E4.

If E1 fails, stop class-exposure search. If E3 fails, stop SAM. If E4 improves final classification, perform only a minimal activity/subject/final representation sanity check; probe improvement without final metric gain is failure. If all training variants fail, stop supervised STR training modifications on this fixed development set. Any later external wearable self-supervised pretraining requires a separate protocol and independent evidence boundary.
